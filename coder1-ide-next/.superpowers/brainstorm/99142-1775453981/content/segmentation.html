What's the quiz's primary job?

This determines what questions to ask and how to route users at the end

A

Segment by use case / persona

Quiz identifies who they are — solo vs team, beginner vs power user, low-volume vs high-volume agent. Email sequence and onboarding are personalized to their persona bucket. Each bucket gets a different nurture sequence with content tailored to their world.

Best for

  • Products with multiple distinct user types
  • Building a segmented email list from day one

Requires

  • 2–3 separate email sequences
  • Clear persona definitions
B

Qualify by pain point

Quiz uncovers what's hurting them right now — their biggest bottleneck, what's costing them time/money, what they've tried before. The email sequence speaks directly to that specific pain. Single sequence, but dynamically references their answer.

Best for

  • Products that solve a universal pain
  • High-trust, consultative sales

Requires

  • Dynamic email personalization
  • Clear pain → solution mapping
C

Score readiness / fit

Quiz calculates a score — "Your AI Readiness Score: 72/100" — that reveals whether they're a good fit now or not yet. High scorers get a direct trial CTA. Low scorers get a nurture sequence to bring them up to speed first.

Best for

  • Products with a learning curve
  • Filtering out bad-fit users who churn

Requires

  • Scoring rubric
  • Two-track flow (ready vs not)
D

Recommend the right plan / tier

Quiz acts as a plan selector — answers map to Free, Pro, or Team tier. End result: "Based on your answers, Pro is your best fit — here's why." Removes decision paralysis, increases plan upgrades.

Best for

  • Products with tiered pricing
  • Reducing free-tier churn

Requires

  • Clear tier differentiation
  • Honest plan matching logic
