What's the quiz's primary job?

This determines what questions to ask and how to route users at the end. You can pick different answers for Coder1 vs Dunn Deal.

A

Segment by persona / use case

Quiz identifies who they are — solo vs team, beginner vs power user, low-volume vs high-volume agent. Email sequence and onboarding are personalized to their persona bucket.

Best for

  • Products with multiple distinct user types
  • Building a segmented email list from day one

Requires

  • 2–3 separate email sequences
  • Clear persona definitions upfront
B

Qualify by pain point

Quiz uncovers what's hurting them right now — their biggest bottleneck, what's costing them time/money, what they've tried before. Email sequence speaks directly to that specific pain.

Best for

  • Products that solve a universal pain
  • High-trust, consultative sales

Requires

  • Dynamic email personalization
  • Clear pain → solution mapping
C

Score readiness / fit

Quiz calculates a score — "Your AI Readiness Score: 72/100" — revealing whether they're a good fit now. High scorers get the trial CTA. Low scorers get a nurture sequence first.

Best for

  • Products with a learning curve or requirements
  • Filtering bad-fit users who churn quickly

Requires

  • Scoring rubric
  • Two-track flow (ready vs. not yet)
D

Recommend the right plan / tier

Quiz acts as a plan selector — answers map to Free, Pro, or Team. End result: "Based on your answers, Pro is the best fit — here's why." Removes decision paralysis.

Best for

  • Products with tiered pricing
  • Reducing free-tier churn / increasing upgrades

Requires

  • Clear tier differentiation
  • Honest plan matching logic
