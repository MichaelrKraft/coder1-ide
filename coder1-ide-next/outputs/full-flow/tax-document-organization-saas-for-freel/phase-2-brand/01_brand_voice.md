# Brand Voice — TaxDrop
## Tax Document Organization SaaS for Freelancers

**Brand Name:** TaxDrop
**Tagline:** Every deduction. Automatically found.
**Brand folder:** `.claude/skills/pptx-generator/brands/taxdrop/`

---

## Personality in 3 Words

**Straight-talking. Financially savvy. Quietly reassuring.**

---

## Core Voice Principles

1. **Lead with the dollar amount.** Every claim connects to money saved or found. "The average TaxDrop user finds $2,800 in deductions they would have missed." Numbers are the headline.

2. **Speak like a financially literate friend, not a software company.** Not formal. Not startup-hype. The friend who actually understands Schedule C and tells you straight whether you're leaving money on the table. Warm but direct.

3. **Respect their intelligence and their independence.** Our customer files their own taxes. They chose not to use a CPA. Don't condescend. Don't over-explain.

4. **Address the fear without amplifying it.** Acknowledge the audit/overpayment fear, then dissolve it with specificity. Move fast to resolution.

---

## Vocabulary

### Words we reach for
- "find" (find deductions, find what you missed)
- "catch" (catch expenses before they slip through)
- "ready to file"
- "Schedule C", "1099", "deduction" — real terms, not softened versions
- "year-round"
- "your money" — possession framing

### Words we avoid
- leverage, seamlessly, unlock, supercharge, game-changer, empower, solution

### Sentence style
**Short punchy declaration + one brief proof sentence.**

> "TurboTax only captures what you tell it. We capture what you forgot."
> "The average Etsy seller misses 14 deductible expenses per year. TaxDrop catches them automatically."

Use contractions freely. Target: 12–18 words for headlines, max 30 words per body sentence.

---

## Objection-Response Patterns

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I can just use TurboTax" | TurboTax captures what you tell it. TaxDrop captures what you forgot. Those are different numbers. |
| "I don't trust connecting my bank" | We use Plaid — read-only, the same connection Venmo and Robinhood use. We see transactions. We can't move money. |
| "I'll do it with a spreadsheet" | You said that last year. You probably missed $2,400. TaxDrop's free to try. |

## Voice Influences

- Ramit Sethi — direct, anti-hype, dollar-anchored
- Navi Maraj CPA — clear financial explanation without condescension
- Linear (product writing) — precise, respects user intelligence
- Wise — transparent, number-forward, anti-jargon
