Chapter Two

The 5 Automations
Every SMB Should Implement First

Not every AI automation is equal. These five deliver ROI faster and more reliably than anything else — and none require a developer.

💬

Customer Support AI

Quick win

Deploy an AI chatbot that handles FAQs, order status, appointment booking, and basic troubleshooting 24/7. Deflects 40–70% of tickets before a human sees them.

Avg ROI: 3–4 months · Tools: Intercom, Tidio, Voiceflow
📧

Lead Follow-Up Sequences

Quick win

Automate personalized email and SMS follow-ups triggered by lead behavior. AI writes the messages based on what the prospect did. Response rates 3–5x higher than manual follow-up.

Avg ROI: 6–8 weeks · Tools: HubSpot, ActiveCampaign + AI
📋

Document & Invoice Processing

Medium effort

AI extracts data from invoices, contracts, and forms — routes it to the right system automatically. Eliminates manual data entry. Average 40+ hours/week reclaimed for businesses with high document volume.

Avg ROI: 3 months · Tools: Zapier + AI, Make.com
✍️

Content Production Pipeline

Quick win

AI drafts blog posts, social media content, email newsletters, and product descriptions from a simple brief. Your team edits and approves. 10x output without 10x headcount.

Avg ROI: Immediate · Tools: Claude, ChatGPT, Jasper
📊

Reporting & Insights

Medium effort

Automated weekly reports that pull from your CRM, sales data, and operations — and summarize what matters in plain English. Decisions get faster. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Avg ROI: 4–6 months · Tools: Power BI + AI, Notion AI

Chapter Four

A 90-Day Roadmap
to Measurable AI ROI

The most successful SMBs follow a structured expansion pattern — not a big-bang rollout. This is the sequence that consistently produces measurable results in 90 days.

1
Week 1–2 · Foundation
Audit and identify your biggest time sinks
Map where your team spends the most hours on repetitive tasks. Use the calculator above. Pick ONE workflow to automate first — the one with the highest time cost. Don't try to do everything at once.
2
Week 2–4 · First Automation
Deploy one automation and track the results
Start with customer support chatbot or lead follow-up — both show ROI within 30 days. Measure: hours saved, response time, conversion rate. Document the before/after. This becomes your internal business case for expanding.
3
Week 4–6 · Team Training
Run a half-day AI training for your core team
Cover: how the tools work, how to write effective prompts for your specific tasks, how to verify outputs, how to identify new use cases. Create 3–5 shared prompt templates the whole team can use immediately.
4
Week 6–10 · Expand
Add two more automations based on measured impact
Now that you have proof of concept and a trained team, expand to the next highest-impact workflows. Content production and document processing typically follow. By now you should have measurable weekly hours saved.
5
Day 90 · Review & Roadmap
Measure, document, plan Year 2
Calculate total hours saved, revenue impact, and error rate reduction. Present findings to the team. Build your 12-month AI roadmap based on what you've learned. By this point, most SMBs are seeing 3–5 hours saved per employee per week — a measurable competitive advantage.
