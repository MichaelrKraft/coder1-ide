Questions You're Probably Asking

I'm not technical at all. Can I really do this?

+

Yes — and in fact, being non-technical is often an advantage. The bottleneck in AI services isn't knowing how to code. It's knowing how a real business operates, how decisions get made, and what problems are worth solving. That's exactly what 10+ years of corporate experience gives you. We teach you the AI part. You already have the hard part.

How many hours per week does this require?

+

Minimum 5–10 hours/week to make meaningful progress. Most successful members start with 10–15 hours while keeping their day job. The payoff: once you have 2–3 retainer clients, you're earning the equivalent of a second salary for 10 hours of work per week, because AI handles the execution.

What if AI keeps improving and makes these skills obsolete too?

+

This is the right question to ask. The answer: the skills we teach are meta-skills — how to identify high-value problems, how to leverage AI for solutions, how to communicate with clients, how to build trust. Those skills get more valuable as AI improves, not less. The person who knows how to use AI to deliver business outcomes is not replaceable by AI itself.

Is the market already saturated with AI freelancers?

+

Not for what we teach. Yes, there are thousands of generic "AI content writers" on Fiverr. That market is commoditized. But the market for domain-expert AI consultants — someone with 10 years in finance who now helps finance teams use AI — is dramatically undersupplied. That's the specific niche we help you occupy, and it commands 5–10x the rates of generic AI services.
