## 📝 Quick Tips

### Demo Video Tips
- **Show Real Usage:** Use actual Coder1, not mockups
- **Start with Pain:** "You've built 5 MVPs that went nowhere..."
- **Be Authentic:** You're a founder, not an actor
- **Clear CTA:** "Apply now, 20 spots only, link in description"

### Alpha Tester Selection Tips
- **Engagement > Skill:** Active feedback matters more than expertise
- **Diversity:** Select different use cases (SaaS, tools, games, etc.)
- **Red Flags:** "I want free stuff", generic answers, unclear goals
- **Green Flags:** Specific problem, clear plan, enthusiasm

### Community Management Tips
- **Be Present:** Respond to messages within 2-4 hours
- **Celebrate Wins:** When someone ships, celebrate loudly
- **Own Issues:** If there's a bug, acknowledge and fix fast
- **Weekly Check-ins:** Scheduled feedback sessions keep engagement high
