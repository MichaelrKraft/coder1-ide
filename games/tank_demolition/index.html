🎮 Tank Demolition

An Atari-Style Tank Game Adventure

✓ Session 2: Shooting Mechanics Complete

🎯 Controls

↑ UP Arrow Move tank forward
↓ DOWN Arrow Move tank backward
← LEFT Arrow Rotate tank left
→ RIGHT Arrow Rotate tank right
A Key Rotate turret left
D Key Rotate turret right
SPACEBAR Fire projectile (max 3)

💡 Tips for Session 2

  • The turret rotates independently from the tank body!
  • You can only have 3 projectiles on screen at once
  • Watch for the muzzle flash when you fire
  • Try moving and shooting at the same time
  • Notice the cool trail effect behind projectiles
